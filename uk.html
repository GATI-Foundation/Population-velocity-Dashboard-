Data Notes & Sources Read before comparing

What each measure counts, where it comes from, and what it should not be compared with.

Stock vs flow
A stock is how many people are present at one moment (like a photo). A flow is how many moved during a year (like a video). Population and the nurse and doctor registers are stocks; arrivals and visas are flows.
Nationality vs country of training
Population, arrivals and visas count Indian nationals. Nurse and doctor counts are by country of training: a British citizen who qualified in India is included, an Indian national trained in the UK is not.
Series break in 2021
ONS long-term migration figures from 2021 use Home Office administrative data. Earlier figures came from a passenger survey and are not comparable, so arrivals start in 2021. ONS figures are provisional and rounded to the nearest 1,000.
Every share uses one source
Each "India's share" divides an Indian figure by a total from the same table, year and definition. Visa shares use main applicants on both sides unless the chart says otherwise.
